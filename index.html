ScanEase Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

ScanEase is a document scanner that works entirely on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. ScanEase has no account, no server and no analytics. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal information or any document content.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos you take or import, the cleaned-up scans, and any PDF, Excel or CSV you create are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Text recognition (OCR) and blur correction run on your device. No image or text is uploaded.
  • You can delete a scan at any time inside the app, or delete everything by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph documents. Used only when you tap Scan.
  • Photos / files: only the images you choose to import, and the location you choose when saving an export.

Sharing

A file leaves the app only when you choose Share or Save. It then goes to the app or location you pick, and that app's own policy applies.

Third-party components

ScanEase uses Google's ML Kit for on-device text recognition and document scanning. These run on the device. On some Android phones, Google Play services may download the ML Kit scanning module; that download does not include your documents.

Children

ScanEase does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
